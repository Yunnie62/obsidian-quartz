## H.A.R.N.E.S.S. Reading Workflow

Use these seven steps for each book or textbook module:

1. **H - Highlight**: Mark only rules, thresholds, exceptions, and examples that matter.
2. **A - Ask**: Turn the text into exam-style questions.
3. **R - Rephrase**: Rewrite the rule in your own words.
4. **N - Network**: Link the idea to modules, topics, examples, and mistakes.
5. **E - Extract**: Convert the idea into reusable rule, calculation, or comparison cards.
6. **S - Synthesize**: Build a one-page module summary.
7. **S - Ship**: Produce a cheat sheet, self-test, explanation, or mistake review.
