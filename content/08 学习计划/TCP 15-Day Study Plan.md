# TCP 15-Day Study Plan

## Goal

Finish the first full learning pass of the TCP textbook within 15 days while working full-time.

This plan is designed for:

- weekdays: about 2 hours per day
- heavier days/weekends: about 3-4 hours
- exercise and work schedule preserved
- question practice after each major TCP unit, not after every small module

## Core Rule

Do not force 50 MCQ and 2 TBS every day.

Instead, finish one major unit, then do a focused practice block:

- TCP 1: 40-50 MCQ + 1-2 TBS
- TCP 2: 50-60 MCQ + 2 TBS
- TCP 3: 40-50 MCQ + 1-2 TBS
- TCP 4: 35-40 MCQ + 1 TBS
- June 2026 Updates: review numbers and changed rules, no heavy separate question block

## Daily Study Template

### Regular Study Day - About 2 Hours

- [ ] 20 min - Read Obsidian module summary
- [ ] 50 min - Read the related PDF pages / examples
- [ ] 30 min - Review rule cards and explain the module out loud
- [ ] 20 min - Light MCQ practice or update mistake notes

### Practice Day - About 2-3 Hours

- [ ] 15 min - Review the unit map
- [ ] 75-90 min - MCQ practice
- [ ] 30-40 min - TBS practice
- [ ] 20 min - Mistake review and update cards
