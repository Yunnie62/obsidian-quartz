；；# TCP 2 Module 4 - International Tax Issues

## Module Purpose

This module introduces transfer pricing, sourcing rules, foreign tax credit, participation exemption, anti-deferral regimes, GILTI, BEAT, FDII, withholding, residency tests, expatriation, and treaties.

## High-Yield Rules

- Transfer pricing uses the arm's-length standard for controlled transactions.
- Sourcing rules determine whether income is U.S.-source or foreign-source.
- Foreign tax credit is limited to the U.S. tax attributable to foreign-source income.
- Participation exemption may allow a 100% DRD for certain foreign-source dividends.
- Subpart F and GILTI can cause current U.S. taxation without an actual dividend.
- BEAT targets large U.S. corporations with significant deductible payments to related foreign affiliates.
- FDII provides a deduction for certain foreign-derived intangible income of U.S. C corporations.
