# TCP 2 - Corporations

## Unit Objective

Understand corporate tax planning, including loss limitations, entity-owner transactions, consolidated returns, international tax issues, corporate planning, and tax effects of formation and liquidation.

## H.A.R.N.E.S.S. for This Unit

### H - Highlight

- NOL and capital loss limitations
- classification of shareholder-corporation transactions
- requirements for consolidated returns
- key definitions and calculations in international tax
- recognition rules for formation and liquidation

### A - Ask

- Does the transaction occur at the corporate level or shareholder level?
- Is gain or loss recognized?
- How does basis change?
- Is there a carryforward or limitation?
