# TCP 4 - Dispositions and Related Party Transactions

## Unit Objective

Understand gain/loss recognition, exclusions and deferrals, trade or business-use property rules, depreciation recapture, and related party transaction limitations.

## H.A.R.N.E.S.S. for This Unit

### H - Highlight

- homeowner's exclusion
- involuntary conversions
- like-kind exchanges
- installment sales
- Section 1231 gains and losses
- Section 1245 and Section 1250 depreciation recapture
- related party constructive ownership
- related party loss disallowance
- below-market loans and imputed interest

### A - Ask

- Is gain recognized, excluded, or deferred?
- Is the property personal-use, capital, business-use, or inventory?
- Is the gain ordinary, capital, Section 1231, or depreciation recapture?
- Is the transaction with a related party?
- Does basis carry over or reset?
