## Unit Objective

Understand advanced tax rules for S corporations, partnerships, trusts, and tax-exempt organizations.

## H.A.R.N.E.S.S. for This Unit

### H - Highlight

- basis calculations
- distribution rules
- liquidation ordering
- income allocation in trust taxation
- limitations and exceptions for tax-exempt organizations

### A - Ask

- Is the entity taxed, or are items passed through to owners?
- How does owner basis change?
- Is the distribution taxable?
- Is the liquidation treated as a regular distribution or liquidating distribution?
