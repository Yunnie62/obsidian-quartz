# TCP 1 Module 2 - Loss Limitations

## Module Purpose

This module explains the four main limitations on an individual taxpayer's ability to deduct business losses: tax basis, at-risk amount, passive activity loss rules, and excess business loss limitation.

## H - Highlight

### Four Loss Limitation Hurdles

1. Tax basis limitation
2. At-risk limitation
3. Passive activity loss (PAL) limitation
4. Excess business loss limitation

### Key Numbers

- Material participation common test: more than 500 hours during the tax year.
- Mom-and-pop rental real estate allowance: up to $25,000.
- Mom-and-pop phase-out starts when AGI exceeds $100,000.
- Mom-and-pop allowance is fully eliminated when AGI exceeds $150,000.
- Real estate professional test: more than 50% of personal services in real estate businesses and more than 750 hours of services.
- 2023 excess business loss threshold: $578,000 for married filing jointly; $289,000 for all other taxpayers.

## A - Ask

- Is the taxpayer's loss limited by tax basis?
- Is the taxpayer economically at risk for the loss?
- Is the activity active, passive, or portfolio?
- Does the taxpayer materially participate?
- Is the activity rental real estate?
- Does the mom-and-pop exception apply?
- Does the excess business loss limitation apply after PAL rules?

## R - Rephrase

1. A loss must clear tax basis before it can be deducted.
2. A loss must also clear the at-risk limitation.
3. Passive losses generally offset only passive income.
4. Certain rental real estate losses can be deducted under the mom-and-pop exception.
5. Real estate professionals can treat rental real estate activities as active if the tests are met.
6. Excess business losses are limited after the passive activity loss rules.

## S - Synthesize

### High-Frequency Rules

- Tax basis and at-risk limits often apply before passive loss limits.
- PALs generally cannot offset active or portfolio income.
- Suspended PALs can become deductible when the taxpayer disposes of the passive activity.
- The excess business loss limitation applies to both active and passive business activities after PAL rules.

### Common Traps

- Skipping directly to PAL without checking tax basis and at-risk amount.
- Treating portfolio income as passive income.
- Forgetting the mom-and-pop phase-out.
- Forgetting that real estate professional status requires both the 50% test and the 750-hour test.
- Applying the excess business loss limitation before PAL rules.
