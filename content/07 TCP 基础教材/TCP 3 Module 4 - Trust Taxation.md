# TCP 3 Module 4 - Trust Taxation

## Module Purpose

This module covers grantor and non-grantor trusts, simple and complex trusts, trust accounting income, trust taxable income, distributable net income (DNI), beneficiary taxable income, income distribution deduction, and trust taxable income after distributions.

## High-Yield Rules

- Trust income is taxed either to the trust or beneficiaries depending on trust type, income type, and distributions.
- Simple trusts must distribute all income annually, cannot distribute corpus, and cannot make charitable contributions.
- Complex trusts are non-grantor trusts that do not meet simple trust requirements.
- DNI limits both beneficiary taxable income and the trust income distribution deduction.
- Trust taxable income is computed after considering the income distribution deduction.
