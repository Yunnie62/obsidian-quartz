# TCP 4 Module 3 - Related Party Transactions

## Module Purpose

This module covers related party definitions, constructive ownership, capital gain and loss rules, basis rules, holding period rules, matching of deductions and income, and below-market loans.

## High-Yield Rules

- Constructive ownership rules can treat stock owned by certain related parties as owned by the taxpayer.
- Losses from sales or exchanges between related parties may be disallowed.
- A later sale by the related buyer can use the disallowed loss to reduce gain, subject to limits.
- Related party rules can change gain character and timing of deductions.
- Below-market loans can create imputed interest.
