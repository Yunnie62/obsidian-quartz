# TCP 3 Module 2 - Partnership Advanced Topics

## Module Purpose

This module covers partnership formation, profits interests, capital interests, partner outside basis, partnership inside basis, debt allocation, built-in gain/loss allocations, loss limitations, tax elections, partner-partnership transactions, guaranteed payments, and nonliquidating distributions.

## High-Yield Rules

- Contributions of property to a partnership are generally nontaxable.
- A profits interest received for services is generally not taxable when received.
- A capital interest received for services is generally taxable compensation.
- Outside basis tracks the partner's basis in the partnership interest.
- Inside basis tracks the partnership's basis in its assets.
- Recourse and nonrecourse debt allocations can affect outside basis and at-risk amount differently.
- Guaranteed payments are deductible by the partnership and taxable to the partner.
- Nonliquidating distributions generally reduce outside basis; cash in excess of basis can trigger gain.
