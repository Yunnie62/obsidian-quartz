# TCP 4 Module 2 - Business-Use Property Dispositions

## Module Purpose

This module covers character of gain or loss, capital and noncapital assets, Section 1231 property, Section 1231 five-year lookback, Section 1245 recapture, Section 1250 recapture, and Section 291 recapture for C corporations.

## High-Yield Rules

- Net Section 1231 losses are ordinary losses.
- Net Section 1231 gains are generally treated as long-term capital gains, subject to the five-year lookback rule.
- Section 1245 recaptures depreciation on business-use personal property as ordinary income to the extent of gain.
- Section 1250 applies to business-use real property and generally recaptures excess depreciation over straight-line.
- Section 291 can create additional ordinary income recapture for C corporations.
