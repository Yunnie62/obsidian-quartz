# TCP 2 Module 6 - Entity Formation and Liquidation

## Module Purpose

This module compares tax characteristics of business entities and explains property contributions, entity-level taxation, owner-level taxation, losses, compensation, operating distributions, liquidating distributions, and entity conversions.

## High-Yield Rules

- C corporations face entity-level tax and potential shareholder-level tax on dividends.
- Flow-through entities generally pass income and loss to owners.
- Individual owners of flow-through entities may qualify for the 20% QBI deduction.
- Dividends may be taxed at 0%, 15%, or 20%, depending on the taxpayer.
- Higher-income taxpayers may owe 3.8% net investment income tax.
- Entity choice affects loss deductibility, owner compensation, fringe benefits, and distributions.
