# TCP 2 Module 5 - Tax Planning for Corporations

## Module Purpose

This module explains corporate tax planning strategies: timing, income shifting, use of NOL and capital loss carryovers, changing tax rates, owner transactions, estimated tax payments, and limits on tax planning.

## High-Yield Rules

- Timing strategies answer when income or deductions should be recognized.
- Income-shifting strategies answer who or where should recognize income.
- Capital loss carryovers are useful when capital gains are available.
- NOL carryovers may be more valuable depending on current and future tax rates.
- Tax planning is limited by statutes, regulations, judicial doctrines, business purpose, and substance.
