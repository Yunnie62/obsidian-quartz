# TCP 2 Module 1 - NOLs and Capital Loss Limitations

## Module Purpose

This module explains corporate NOL utilization, Section 382 ownership-change limitations, and corporate capital loss carryover rules.

## High-Yield Rules

- Post-2017 NOL carryforwards can generally be carried forward indefinitely.
- Starting in 2021, post-2017 NOL carryforwards can offset only 80% of taxable income after pre-2018 NOL carryforwards.
- Section 382 limits use of pre-change NOLs after an ownership change.
- Corporate capital losses can offset only capital gains.
