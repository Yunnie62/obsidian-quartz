# TCP 4 Module 1 - Nontaxable Dispositions

## Module Purpose

This module covers gain/loss calculation, exclusions and deferrals, homeowner's exclusion, involuntary conversions, divorce property settlements, like-kind exchanges, installment sales, and corporate treasury/capital stock transactions.

## High-Yield Rules

- Gain or loss equals amount realized minus adjusted basis.
- Home sale gain exclusion is up to $250,000 for single/MFS/HOH and $500,000 for MFJ or certain surviving spouses.
- The home sale exclusion generally requires ownership and use as a principal residence for at least two years during the five-year period ending on sale.
- Like-kind exchange replacement property must be identified within 45 days and received by the earlier of 180 days or the tax return due date with extensions.
- Boot received in a like-kind exchange can trigger recognized gain.
- Installment sale gain is recognized as cash is received using the gross profit percentage.
