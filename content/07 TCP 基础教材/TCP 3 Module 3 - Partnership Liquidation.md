# TCP 3 Module 3 - Partnership Liquidation

## Module Purpose

This module covers liquidating distributions, multiple-asset basis allocation, sale of partnership interests, hot assets, income allocation, retirement or death of a partner, and Section 754 elections.

## High-Yield Rules

- Liquidating distributions terminate the partner's interest.
- Basis is generally allocated first to cash, then hot assets, then other property.
- Sale of a partnership interest generally creates capital gain or loss, except amounts attributable to hot assets.
- Hot assets can convert part of the gain to ordinary income.
- A Section 754 election can allow optional basis adjustments after transfers of partnership interests.
