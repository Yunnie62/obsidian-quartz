# TCP 2 Module 2 - Entity Owner Transactions

## Module Purpose

This module covers C corporation formation, Section 351, corporate distributions, stock redemptions, liquidations, reorganizations, Section 1244 stock, QSBS, and shareholder-corporation loans.

## High-Yield Rules

- Section 351 generally makes corporate formation nontaxable when requirements are met.
- Transferors must control at least 80% of voting stock and 80% of nonvoting stock immediately after the exchange.
- Boot can trigger recognized gain.
- Liabilities in excess of basis can trigger gain.
- Dividends are distributions out of earnings and profits (E&P).
- Section 1244 may allow ordinary loss treatment up to $50,000, or $100,000 for MFJ.
- QSBS held more than five years may qualify for gain exclusion.
