# TCP 3 Module 1 - S Corporation Advanced Topics

## Module Purpose

This module covers S corporation formation, property contributions, corporation-level taxes, shareholder stock and debt basis, loss limitations, per-share per-day allocations, AAA, nonliquidating distributions, termination of S status, and liquidation.

## High-Yield Rules

- S corporation property contributions generally follow Section 351.
- Certain S corporations can still owe corporation-level taxes, including LIFO recapture tax, built-in gains tax, and tax on excess passive investment income.
- Shareholder loss deductions are limited by stock basis, debt basis, at-risk rules, and passive activity rules.
- S corporation income and loss are generally allocated on a per-share, per-day basis.
- AAA tracks accumulated S corporation earnings and affects distribution ordering.
- S status may terminate if eligibility requirements fail or excessive passive investment income rules are triggered.
