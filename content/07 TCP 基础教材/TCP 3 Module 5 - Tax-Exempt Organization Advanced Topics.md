# TCP 3 Module 5 - Tax-Exempt Organization Advanced Topics

## Module Purpose

This module covers Section 501(c)(3) qualification, private benefit restrictions, political and lobbying restrictions, loss of tax-exempt status, and unrelated business income (UBI).

## High-Yield Rules

- A Section 501(c)(3) organization must be a corporation, unincorporated association, or trust.
- It must have an exempt purpose.
- No private benefit is allowed.
- Political campaign activity is prohibited.
- Substantial lobbying can jeopardize exempt status.
- UBI can be taxable even when the organization itself is tax-exempt.
