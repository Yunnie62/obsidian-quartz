## Learning Principle

Understand the textbook by module first, then extract reusable cards. Do not copy full pages into notes. Keep only what helps you solve questions, explain rules, or avoid mistakes.

## Completion Standard for Each Module

- one module summary
- 5-15 rule or calculation cards
- at least 3 common traps
- one self-test set
- ability to explain the module without opening the textbook
