# Timing of Income and Deductions

## Core Rule

Tax planning often compares whether income or deductions should be accelerated or deferred. The best answer depends on tax rates and the time value of money.

## Income Timing

- Deferring income is generally beneficial when tax rates are constant because tax is paid later.
- Accelerating income may be beneficial if the current tax rate is lower than the future tax rate.

## Deduction Timing

- Accelerating deductions is generally beneficial when tax rates are constant because tax savings arrive earlier.
- Deferring deductions may be beneficial if the future tax rate is meaningfully higher.

## Calculation Pattern

1. Compute tax cost or tax savings.
2. Apply the present value factor if the tax effect occurs in a future year.
3. Compare after-tax income or after-tax cost.
