# Recourse vs Nonrecourse Debt Allocation

## Recourse Debt

Recourse debt is generally allocated to partners who bear the economic risk of loss.

## Nonrecourse Debt

Nonrecourse debt is generally allocated according to partnership sharing rules because no partner bears personal liability.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：debt allocation 会影响 outside basis，但 at-risk treatment 可能不同。
