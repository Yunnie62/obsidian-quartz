# Corporate Income Shifting

## Core Idea

Income-shifting strategies decide who or where should recognize income. They may involve jurisdictions, related parties, owner compensation, transfer pricing, or entity choice.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：income shifting 一定要考虑 arm's-length、reasonable compensation 和 business purpose。
