# Below-Market Loans in Related Party Transactions

## Core Rule

Section 7872 can recharacterize a below-market loan as an arm's-length loan with imputed interest, causing tax consequences to both lender and borrower.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：below-market loan 的税务结果是“视同有 interest”，不是只看实际付款。
