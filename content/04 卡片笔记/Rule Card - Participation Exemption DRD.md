# Participation Exemption DRD

## Core Rule

The participation exemption may allow a 100% dividends-received deduction for certain foreign-source dividends paid to a U.S. C corporation by a specified foreign corporation.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：100% DRD 不代表所有 foreign income 都免税，Subpart F、GILTI 等可能不适用。
