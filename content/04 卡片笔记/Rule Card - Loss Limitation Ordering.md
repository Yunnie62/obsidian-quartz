# Loss Limitation Ordering

## Core Rule

Individual business losses must pass through four limitations before they are deductible.

## Ordering

1. Tax basis limitation
2. At-risk limitation
3. Passive activity loss limitation
4. Excess business loss limitation

## Exam Approach

1. Start with the total allocated loss.
2. Limit the loss to tax basis.
3. Limit the remaining loss to at-risk amount.
4. Determine whether the loss is passive.
5. Apply the excess business loss limitation after PAL rules.
