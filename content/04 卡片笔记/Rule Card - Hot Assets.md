# Hot Assets

## Core Rule

Hot assets are partnership assets that generate ordinary income rather than capital gain, such as inventory and unrealized receivables.

## Exam Impact

Hot assets can cause part of the gain on sale of a partnership interest to be ordinary income.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：partnership interest sale 通常 capital，但 hot assets 会切出 ordinary income。
