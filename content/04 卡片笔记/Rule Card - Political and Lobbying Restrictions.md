# Political and Lobbying Restrictions

## Core Rule

Section 501(c)(3) organizations cannot participate in political campaign activity, and substantial lobbying can jeopardize exempt status.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：nonpartisan voter education may be different from prohibited campaign intervention。
