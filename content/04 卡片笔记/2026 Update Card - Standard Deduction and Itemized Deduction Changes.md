# 2026 Update - Standard Deduction and Itemized Deduction Changes

## 2026 Standard Deduction

- Single: $16,100
- Head of household: $24,150
- MFJ or surviving spouse: $32,200
- MFS: $16,100

## Key Itemized Deduction Updates

- SALT limit: $40,400 in 2026.
- Cash charitable deduction for nonitemizers: $1,000, or $2,000 MFJ.
- Charitable contributions: 0.5% of AGI floor.
