# Estate Planning Ownership and Beneficiary Designations

## Core Rule

Estate planning depends on legal ownership and beneficiary designations. Some assets transfer directly outside probate.

## Key Ideas

- Wills create a plan for probate assets.
- Beneficiary designations can control retirement accounts, life insurance, brokerage accounts, and bank accounts.
- Joint tenancy with right of survivorship can transfer assets directly to the surviving owner.
