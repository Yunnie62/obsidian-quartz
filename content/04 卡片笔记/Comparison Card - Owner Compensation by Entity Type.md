# Owner Compensation by Entity Type

## C and S Corporations

Shareholder-employees may receive salaries. Compensation must generally be reasonable.

## Partnerships

Partners may receive guaranteed payments rather than employee wages.

## Sole Proprietorships

The owner generally does not receive wages from the sole proprietorship.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：owner 拿钱的形式取决于 entity type，salary、guaranteed payment、distribution 不要混。
