# Partnership Inside Basis

## Core Rule

Inside basis is the partnership's basis in its assets. When property is contributed, the partnership generally takes a carryover basis in the contributed property.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：inside basis 用来看 partnership 持有资产的 basis，不是 partner 的 outside basis。
