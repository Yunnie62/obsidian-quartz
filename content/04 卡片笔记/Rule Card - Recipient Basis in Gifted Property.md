# Recipient Basis in Gifted Property

## Core Rule

The recipient of a gift generally does not recognize taxable income when receiving the gift.

## Basis Rule

The general basis rule for gifted property is carryover basis: the recipient's basis equals the donor's basis, adjusted for gift tax paid attributable to appreciation when applicable.

- 易错提醒：recipient 收到 gift 通常不确认 income，但以后出售时 basis 很重要。
