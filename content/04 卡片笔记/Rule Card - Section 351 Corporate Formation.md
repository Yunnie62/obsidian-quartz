# Section 351 Corporate Formation

## Core Rule

Formation of a corporation is generally nontaxable if property is transferred to the corporation solely in exchange for stock and the transferors control the corporation immediately after the exchange.

## Control Requirement

The transferors must own at least 80% of voting stock and at least 80% of nonvoting stock immediately after the exchange.
