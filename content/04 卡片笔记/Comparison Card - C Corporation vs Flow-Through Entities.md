# C Corporation vs Flow-Through Entities

## C Corporation

- Entity-level tax applies.
- Shareholder-level tax may apply when earnings are distributed as dividends.
- Certain fringe benefits may be more favorable.

## Flow-Through Entities

- Income and losses generally pass through to owners.
- Individual owners may qualify for the 20% QBI deduction.
- Losses may be limited by basis, at-risk, passive activity, and excess business loss rules.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：C corp 的关键词是 double taxation；flow-through 的关键词是 pass-through and owner-level limitations。
