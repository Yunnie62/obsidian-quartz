# 2026 Update - Trump Accounts

## Core Rule

Trump accounts are tax-deferred investment accounts for children under age 18 with a valid Social Security number.

## Key Limits

- Available starting July 4, 2026.
- Pilot federal contribution: $1,000 for qualifying U.S. citizen children born January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028.
- Employer contribution limit: $2,500 per year.
- Maximum total contribution: $5,000 per year.
- No deduction for contributions.
- No distributions before the calendar year the beneficiary turns 18.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：Trump account is treated like a traditional IRA after age 18, but has special child-account restrictions before then.
