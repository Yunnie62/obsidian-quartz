# Partnership Nonliquidating Distributions

## Core Rule

Nonliquidating distributions generally reduce the partner's outside basis. Cash distributions in excess of outside basis can trigger gain.

## Basis Allocation

Basis is generally assigned first to cash, then hot assets, then other property.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：cash distribution 可能触发 gain；property distribution 通常先看 basis allocation。
