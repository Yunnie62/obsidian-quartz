# 2026 Update - IRA and RMD Rules

## IRA Contribution Limits

- Under age 50: $7,500
- Age 50 or older: $8,600

## RMD Age

Traditional 401(k) RMDs begin by April 1 of the year after the later of reaching:

- age 73, if born between 1951 and 1959;
- age 75, if born in 1960 or later; or
- terminating employment with the plan sponsor.

## RMD Penalty

- 25%
- reduced to 10% if corrected timely

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：Roth 401(k) is not subject to these RMD requirements.
