# Present Interest vs Future Interest Gifts

## Present Interest

A present interest gives the donee a current right to use, possess, or enjoy the property. It generally qualifies for the annual exclusion.

## Future Interest

A future interest delays the donee's right to use, possess, or enjoy the property. It generally does not qualify for the annual exclusion.

## Exam Approach

1. Identify what the donee can do now.
2. Determine whether enjoyment is postponed.
3. Apply or deny the annual exclusion.
