# Traditional IRA vs. Roth IRA: the core difference is tax timing

## Traditional IRA

- Contributions may be deductible.
- Distributions often include ordinary income.
- The key issue is whether contributions were deductible or nondeductible.

## Roth IRA

- Contributions are not deductible when made.
- Qualified distributions may be tax-free.
- The key issue is whether the distribution is qualified.

## Exam Approach

Identify the account type first, then determine whether the contribution was deductible, and finally determine the tax treatment of principal and earnings in the distribution.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：Traditional IRA 看“现在是否扣除”，Roth IRA 看“以后是否免税”。
