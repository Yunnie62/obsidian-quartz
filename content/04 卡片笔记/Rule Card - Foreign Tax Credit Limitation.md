# Foreign Tax Credit Limitation

## Core Rule

The foreign tax credit is limited so the credit does not exceed the U.S. tax attributable to foreign-source taxable income.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：FTC 是 limited credit，不是 foreign tax paid 全额都能抵。
