# BEAT and FDII Overview

## BEAT

BEAT is a minimum tax aimed at large U.S. corporations with significant deductible payments to related foreign affiliates.

## FDII

FDII provides a deduction for certain foreign-derived intangible income earned by U.S. C corporations.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：BEAT is defensive anti-base-erosion; FDII is incentive-style export/intangible treatment.
