# Corporate Timing Strategies

## Core Idea

Timing strategies decide when income, deductions, NOLs, or capital losses should be recognized or used. The answer depends on tax rates, present value, carryovers, and business constraints.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：timing strategy 要看 tax rate changes 和 present value，不是机械地 defer income。
