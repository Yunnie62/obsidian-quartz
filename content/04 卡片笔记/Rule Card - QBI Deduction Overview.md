# QBI Deduction Overview

## Core Rule

The QBI deduction is generally available to individual owners of flow-through entities and is generally 20% of qualified business income, subject to limitations.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：QBI 是 owner-level deduction，不是 entity-level deduction。
