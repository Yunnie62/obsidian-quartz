# Section 382 Ownership Change Limitation

## Core Rule

Section 382 limits the use of pre-change NOLs after a loss corporation has an ownership change. The rule prevents profitable corporations from buying loss corporations mainly to use their NOL carryforwards.

## Exam Approach

1. Identify whether the corporation is a loss corporation.
2. Determine whether an ownership change occurred.
3. Apply the Section 382 annual limitation to pre-change losses.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：Section 382 不是取消 NOL，而是限制每年能用多少。
