# S Corporation AAA and Distribution Ordering

## Core Rule

The accumulated adjustments account (AAA) tracks accumulated S corporation earnings. Distribution ordering depends on whether the S corporation has accumulated C corporation E&P.

## Key Idea

AAA helps determine whether distributions are tax-free recovery of basis, dividends from C corporation E&P, or capital gain.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：有 C corp E&P 时，S corp distribution ordering 会更复杂。
