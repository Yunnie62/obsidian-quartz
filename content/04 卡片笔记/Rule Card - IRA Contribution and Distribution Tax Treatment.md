# IRA Contribution and Distribution Tax Treatment

## 2023 Contribution Limits

- Under age 50: $6,500.
- Age 50 or older: $7,500.
- The limit applies to the combined total of traditional and Roth IRA contributions.

## Distribution Treatment

- Nondeductible traditional IRA distribution: principal nontaxable, earnings taxable.
- Deductible traditional IRA distribution: principal taxable, earnings taxable.
- Qualified Roth IRA distribution: principal nontaxable, earnings nontaxable.
- Nonqualified Roth IRA distribution: principal nontaxable, earnings taxable.
