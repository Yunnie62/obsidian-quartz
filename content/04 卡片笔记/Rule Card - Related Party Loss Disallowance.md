# Related Party Loss Disallowance

## Core Rule

Losses from sales or exchanges between related parties are generally disallowed.

## Later Sale

If the related buyer later sells the property at a gain, the previously disallowed loss may reduce the buyer's gain, subject to limits.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：related-party loss is disallowed now, but may affect later gain calculation.
