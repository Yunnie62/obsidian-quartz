# Partnership Outside Basis

## Core Rule

Outside basis is the partner's basis in the partnership interest. It is used to determine loss deductibility, gain on distributions, and gain or loss on sale of the partnership interest.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：outside basis 属于 partner；inside basis 属于 partnership assets。
