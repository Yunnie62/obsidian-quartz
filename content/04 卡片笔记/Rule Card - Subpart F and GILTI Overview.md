# Subpart F and GILTI Overview

## Core Rule

Subpart F and GILTI are anti-deferral regimes that can require U.S. shareholders of controlled foreign corporations to include certain foreign income currently.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：没有实际 dividend，也可能有 current U.S. taxable inclusion。
