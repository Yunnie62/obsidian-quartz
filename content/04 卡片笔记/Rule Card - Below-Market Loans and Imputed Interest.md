# Below-Market Loans and Imputed Interest

## Core Rule

When a loan charges no interest or interest below the applicable federal rate, tax law may impute interest. The foregone interest is treated as if it had been paid and then transferred back between the parties.

## Loans Commonly Tested

- Gift loans
- Compensation-related loans
- Corporation-shareholder loans
- Tax-avoidance loans
- Certain loans involving qualified continuing care facilities

## Exam Approach

1. Identify the relationship between lender and borrower.
2. Determine whether the loan is below-market.
3. Check whether an exception applies.
4. Calculate or identify foregone interest.
5. Determine the income, deduction, compensation, dividend, or gift consequence.

## High-Yield Exceptions

- Certain loans of $10,000 or less may qualify for a de minimis exception.
- Gift loans of $100,000 or less may have a limitation based on the borrower's net investment income.
- Exceptions do not apply when tax avoidance is a main purpose.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：below-market loan 的重点不是“有没有真的付利息”，而是税法是否把利息视同发生。
