# AMT starts with regular taxable income and adjusts to AMTI

## Core Formula

Regular taxable income  
+ AMT adjustments  
+ AMT preference items  
= AMTI

## Exam Approach

- Determine whether the item is an adjustment or a preference item.
- Watch for timing differences.
- Some regular tax deductions are not allowed for AMT.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：AMT 不是重新算一套收入，而是从 regular taxable income 出发做调整。
