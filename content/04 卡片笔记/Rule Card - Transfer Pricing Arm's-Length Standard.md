# Transfer Pricing Arm's-Length Standard

## Core Rule

Controlled transactions must be priced as if unrelated parties were dealing at arm's length. The IRS may adjust income, deductions, credits, or allowances to reflect true taxable income.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：transfer pricing 的核心是 controlled transaction 是否像 unrelated parties 一样定价。
