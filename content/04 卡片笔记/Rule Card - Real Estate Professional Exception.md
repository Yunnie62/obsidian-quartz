# Real Estate Professional Exception

## Core Rule

If the taxpayer qualifies as a real estate professional, rental real estate activities are treated as active rather than passive, and the PAL limitation does not apply.

## Tests

The taxpayer must satisfy both tests:

- More than 50% of the taxpayer's personal services during the year are performed in real estate businesses.
- The taxpayer performs more than 750 hours of services in real estate businesses during the year.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：real estate professional 必须两个条件都满足，不能只看 750 hours。
