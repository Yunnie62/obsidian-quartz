# Profits Interest vs Capital Interest

## Profits Interest

A profits interest gives the partner a right to share in future profits or losses. A profits interest received for services is generally not taxable when received.

## Capital Interest

A capital interest gives the partner a right to share in partnership net assets upon liquidation. A capital interest received for services is generally taxable compensation.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：profits interest 看未来利润；capital interest 看现在清算能拿到的资产份额。
