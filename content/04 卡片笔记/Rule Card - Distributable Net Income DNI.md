# Distributable Net Income (DNI)

## Core Rule

DNI limits both the amount beneficiaries include in taxable income and the amount the trust can deduct as an income distribution deduction.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：DNI 是 trust deduction 和 beneficiary income 的共同上限。
