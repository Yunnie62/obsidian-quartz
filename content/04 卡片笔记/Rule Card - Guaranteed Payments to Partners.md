# Guaranteed Payments to Partners

## Core Rule

Guaranteed payments are payments to partners for services or use of capital made without regard to the partnership's income. They are deductible by the partnership and taxable to the partner.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：guaranteed payment 不是 profit distribution，它像 partner 的固定补偿。
