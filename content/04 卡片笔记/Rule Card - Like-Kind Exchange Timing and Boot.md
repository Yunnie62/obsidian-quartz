# Like-Kind Exchange Timing and Boot

## Core Rule

Like-kind exchange treatment can defer gain for qualifying business or investment real property.

## Timing Requirements

- Replacement property must be identified within 45 days.
- Replacement property must be received by the earlier of 180 days after transfer or the tax return due date, including extensions.

## Boot

Boot, such as cash, debt relief, or nonqualifying property, can trigger recognized gain.

- 易错提醒：like-kind exchange 现在重点是 real property，且 45/180 天必须记牢。
