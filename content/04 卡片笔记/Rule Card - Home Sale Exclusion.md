# Home Sale Exclusion

## Core Rule

Gain from the sale of a principal residence may be excluded up to $250,000 for single, married filing separately, and head of household taxpayers, or $500,000 for married filing jointly and certain surviving spouses.

## Qualification

The taxpayer generally must have owned and used the property as a principal residence for at least two years during the five-year period ending on the sale date.
