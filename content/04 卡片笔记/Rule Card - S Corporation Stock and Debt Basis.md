# S Corporation Stock and Debt Basis

## Core Rule

S corporation shareholders track both stock basis and debt basis. Loss deductions are limited by basis and other limitation rules.

## Stock Basis Increases

- Initial contributions
- Additional contributions
- Ordinary business income
- Separately stated income and gain
- Tax-exempt income

## Stock Basis Decreases

- Distributions
- Nondeductible expenses
- Separately stated losses and deductions
- Ordinary business loss

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：S corp loss 先看 stock basis，再看 debt basis 和其他 limitations。
