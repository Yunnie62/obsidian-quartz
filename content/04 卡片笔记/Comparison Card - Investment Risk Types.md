# Investment Risk Types

## Nonsystematic Risk

Risk unique to a company or industry. It can generally be reduced through diversification.

## Systematic Risk

Market-wide risk that affects the overall market. It cannot be eliminated through diversification.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：diversification 主要降低 nonsystematic risk，不消除 systematic risk。
