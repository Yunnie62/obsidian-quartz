# Mom-and-Pop Rental Real Estate Exception

## Core Rule

An individual taxpayer may deduct up to $25,000 of net passive losses from rental real estate if the taxpayer actively participates and owns at least 10% of the rental real estate activity.

## Phase-Out

- The $25,000 allowance is reduced by 50% of AGI over $100,000.
- The allowance is fully eliminated when AGI exceeds $150,000.

## Exam Approach

1. Confirm the activity is rental real estate.
2. Confirm active participation.
3. Confirm at least 10% ownership.
4. Apply the AGI phase-out.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：mom-and-pop 是 active participation，不是 material participation。要求较低，但有 AGI phase-out。
