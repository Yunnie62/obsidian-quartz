# Operating vs Liquidating Distributions

## Operating Distributions

Operating distributions occur while the entity continues to exist. Tax treatment depends on entity type, E&P, basis, and property distributed.

## Liquidating Distributions

Liquidating distributions occur when the owner or entity interest is being terminated. They often require final gain/loss and basis analysis.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：distribution 题先判断 operating 还是 liquidating，再判断 entity type。
