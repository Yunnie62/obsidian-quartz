# Related Party Constructive Ownership

## Core Rule

Constructive ownership rules can treat stock owned by certain family members, entities, or related parties as owned by the taxpayer.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：related party 不只看直接 ownership，还要看 attribution。
