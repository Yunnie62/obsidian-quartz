# Limits on Tax Planning

## Core Rule

Tax planning strategies are limited by statutes, regulations, disclosure requirements, judicial doctrines, economic substance, business purpose, and anti-abuse rules.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：tax planning 合法不等于任意安排，题目常用 substance/business purpose 卡答案。
