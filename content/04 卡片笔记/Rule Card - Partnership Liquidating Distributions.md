# Partnership Liquidating Distributions

## Core Rule

A liquidating distribution terminates the partner's interest in the partnership. The partner's remaining outside basis is allocated to assets received.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：liquidating distribution 的目标是把 outside basis 用完并结束 partner interest。
