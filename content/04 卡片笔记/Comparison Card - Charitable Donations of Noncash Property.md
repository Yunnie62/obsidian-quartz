# Charitable Donations of Noncash Property

## Ordinary Income Property

Deduction is generally the lesser of adjusted basis or FMV.

Examples include:

- inventory
- short-term assets
- depreciated investment or personal-use property
- depreciation recapture portion of business-use property

## Long-Term Capital Gain Property

Deduction is generally FMV if the property is appreciated capital gain property held for more than one year.

## Tangible Personal Property - Unrelated Use

If tangible personal property is used by the charity for an unrelated purpose, the deduction may be limited to adjusted basis.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：不是所有捐赠都按 FMV 扣。先判断 ordinary income property 还是 LTCG property。
