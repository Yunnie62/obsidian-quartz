# Kiddie Tax

## Core Rule

The net unearned income of certain dependent children is taxed at the parents' tax rate.

## Who Is Commonly Covered

- A dependent child under age 18.
- A child age 18 through under 24 who is a full-time student and does not provide more than half of their own support.

## Exam Approach

1. Determine whether the child is subject to kiddie tax.
2. Separate earned income from unearned income.
3. Compute net unearned income.
4. Tax the appropriate amount at the parents' rate.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：kiddie tax 主要针对 unearned income，不是孩子所有收入都按父母税率。
