# Estimated Tax Safe Harbors

## Core Rule

Taxpayers may owe an underpayment penalty if they do not pay enough tax during the year through withholding or timely estimated tax payments.

## Safe Harbors

The penalty can generally be avoided if:

- the filed return shows less than $1,000 of tax owed; or
- the taxpayer paid the lesser of 90% of current-year tax or 100% of prior-year tax.

## Higher-Income Rule

Use 110% of prior-year tax instead of 100% when prior-year AGI exceeds $150,000.

## Failure-to-Pay Penalty

The failure-to-pay penalty is generally 0.5% of unpaid tax per month, up to 25%.

- 易错提醒：看 prior-year AGI 是否超过 $150,000，超过时 prior-year safe harbor 变成 110%。
