# Corporate Estimated Tax Payments

## Core Rule

Corporations generally must make estimated tax payments during the year and may face penalties for underpayment unless they satisfy an available safe method.

- 易错提醒：corporate estimated tax 题要看 current-year tax、prior-year tax 和 annualized income method。
