# Charitable Contribution AGI Limits

## Core Limits

- Cash contributions to public charities: generally 60% of AGI.
- Ordinary income property: generally 50% of AGI.
- Long-term capital gain property: generally 30% of AGI.

## Carryforward

Excess charitable contributions may be carried forward for five years.

## Exam Approach

1. Classify the contribution.
2. Determine the deductible amount before AGI limits.
3. Apply the correct AGI limitation.
4. Track any carryforward.
