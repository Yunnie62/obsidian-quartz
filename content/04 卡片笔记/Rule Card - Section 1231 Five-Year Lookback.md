# Section 1231 Five-Year Lookback

## Core Rule

Net Section 1231 gains are generally long-term capital gains, but the five-year lookback rule can recharacterize current Section 1231 gain as ordinary income to the extent of prior net Section 1231 losses deducted as ordinary losses.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：1231 gain 不是自动 capital gain，要先 look back 五年。
