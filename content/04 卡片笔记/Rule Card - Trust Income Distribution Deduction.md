# Trust Income Distribution Deduction

## Core Rule

A trust may deduct income distributed or required to be distributed to beneficiaries, limited by DNI and other trust rules.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：trust distribution deduction 防止同一收入在 trust 和 beneficiary 双重纳税。
