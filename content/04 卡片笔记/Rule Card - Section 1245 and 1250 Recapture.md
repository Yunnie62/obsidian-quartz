# Section 1245 and 1250 Recapture

## Section 1245

Applies mainly to depreciable personal property. Gain is recaptured as ordinary income to the extent of depreciation taken.

## Section 1250

Applies to depreciable real property. Recapture generally focuses on depreciation in excess of straight-line depreciation.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：1245 常见于 personal property；1250 常见于 real property。
