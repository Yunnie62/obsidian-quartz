# After-Tax Return on Investment

## ROI Formula

ROI = (Investment sales price - Cost of investment) / Cost of investment

## After-Tax Rate of Return

After-tax return adjusts the investment return for taxes.

## Exam Approach

1. Compute pre-tax return.
2. Identify the applicable tax rate.
3. Reduce the return by the tax effect.
4. Compare investments on an after-tax basis.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：投资回报不能只看 pre-tax ROI，税率会改变真正收益。
