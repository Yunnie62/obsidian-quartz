# Equity Compensation Tax Timing

## Core Rule

The tax treatment of equity compensation depends on the type of award and the relevant date: grant date, vesting date, exercise date, and sale date.

## Key Award Types

- Nonstatutory stock options
- Incentive stock options
- Employee stock purchase plans
- Restricted stock
- Restricted stock units
- Stock appreciation rights

## Exam Approach

1. Identify the award type.
2. Determine whether value is readily determinable.
3. Locate the taxable event.
4. Classify the income as ordinary compensation income or capital gain/loss.
5. Determine basis and holding period.

## High-Yield Notes

- Nonstatutory options can create ordinary compensation income.
- Statutory options may defer regular taxable income, but ISO exercise can create AMT consequences.
- Restricted stock generally creates income when restrictions lapse unless a special election applies.
- Later sale of stock may create capital gain or loss based on adjusted basis.
