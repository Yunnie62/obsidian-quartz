# Section 1244 and QSBS

## Section 1244

Original small business stockholders may receive ordinary loss treatment up to $50,000, or $100,000 for married filing jointly.

## QSBS

A noncorporate shareholder who holds qualified small business stock for more than five years may generally exclude 100% of gain, subject to limits.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：Section 1244 helps losses; QSBS helps gains.
