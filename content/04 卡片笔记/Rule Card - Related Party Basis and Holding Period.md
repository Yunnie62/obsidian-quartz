# Related Party Basis and Holding Period

## Core Rule

Related party transactions can alter basis, gain/loss recognition, holding period, and timing of deductions and income.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：related party 题经常不是问有没有 gain，而是问 character、basis、timing。
