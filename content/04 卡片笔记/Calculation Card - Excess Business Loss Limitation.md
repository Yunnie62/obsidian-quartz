# Excess Business Loss Limitation

## Core Rule

Taxpayers cannot deduct an overall excess business loss for the year. The limitation applies after the passive activity loss rules.

## 2023 Thresholds

- Married filing jointly: $578,000.
- All other taxpayers: $289,000.

## Carryforward

The excess business loss is carried forward as a net operating loss (NOL).

## Exam Approach

1. Apply tax basis, at-risk, and PAL limitations first.
2. Combine business income and business losses.
3. Apply the threshold amount.
4. Carry forward the excess as an NOL.
