# Traditional vs Roth Retirement Accounts

## Traditional

- Contributions may be deductible or made pre-tax.
- Earnings grow tax-deferred.
- Distributions are generally taxable as ordinary income to the extent they represent deductible contributions and earnings.

## Roth

- Contributions are made after tax.
- Qualified distributions can be tax-free.
- Roth treatment is most valuable when future tax rates are expected to be higher.

## Exam Approach

1. Identify the account type.
2. Determine contribution tax treatment.
3. Determine whether the distribution is qualified.
4. Separate principal and earnings if needed.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：Traditional 是 “tax benefit now”；Roth 是 “tax benefit later”。
