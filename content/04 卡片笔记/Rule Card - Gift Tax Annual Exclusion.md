# Gift Tax Annual Exclusion

## Core Rule

The donor may exclude the first $17,000 of gifts made to each donee in 2023.

## Gift Splitting

A gift by either spouse may be treated as made one-half by each spouse. This can create a $34,000 exclusion per donee.

## Limitation

The annual exclusion is generally not available for a gift of a future interest.

- 易错提醒：annual exclusion 是 per donee，不是 donor 一年总共只有 $17,000。
