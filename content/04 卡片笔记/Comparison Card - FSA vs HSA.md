# FSA vs HSA

## Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

- Funded through salary reduction.
- Used for qualified medical or dependent care expenses.
- Generally subject to use-it-or-lose-it rules.
- Unused amounts may be forfeited unless limited carryover or grace-period rules apply.

## Health Savings Account (HSA)

- Used with a high-deductible health plan.
- Contributions can be made on a pre-tax basis.
- Funds can accumulate if not spent.
- Qualified medical withdrawals are tax-favored.

## 2023 HSA Limits

- Individual: $3,850.
- Family: $7,750.
- Additional amount for age 55 or older: $1,000.
