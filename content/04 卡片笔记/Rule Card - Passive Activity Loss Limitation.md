# Passive Activity Loss Limitation

## Core Rule

A passive activity loss can generally offset only passive activity income. It cannot offset active income or portfolio income.

## Income Categories

- Active income: wages, business income from material participation.
- Passive income: income from activities in which the taxpayer does not materially participate.
- Portfolio income: dividends, interest, capital gains, and similar investment income.

## Material Participation

A common material participation test is participation for more than 500 hours during the tax year.

## Suspended Losses

PALs that cannot be used are suspended and carried forward. When the taxpayer disposes of the passive activity, remaining suspended PALs may become deductible.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：dividends 和 capital gains 通常是 portfolio income，不是 passive income。
