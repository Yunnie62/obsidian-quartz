# S Corporation Built-In Gains Tax

## Core Rule

An S corporation may owe corporate-level built-in gains tax when a former C corporation sells or distributes appreciated assets after electing S status.

## Trigger

Built-in gain exists when, on the S election date, the FMV of corporate assets exceeds their adjusted basis.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：S corp 通常 pass-through，但 former C corp 的 built-in gain 可能还会被 corporate-level tax 抓住。
