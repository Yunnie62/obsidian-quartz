# Involuntary Conversions

## Core Rule

Gain from an involuntary conversion can be deferred when the taxpayer reinvests in similar replacement property within the required period.

## Basis Idea

Basis in replacement property generally equals cost of replacement property minus deferred gain.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：involuntary conversion rules defer gains; losses are generally recognized.
