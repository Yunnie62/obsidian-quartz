# Standard Deduction vs Itemized Deduction

## Core Rule

Taxpayers generally choose the larger benefit between the standard deduction and allowable itemized deductions.

## 2023 Standard Deduction Amounts

- Single: $13,850.
- Head of household: $20,800.
- Married filing jointly or surviving spouse: $27,700.
- Married filing separately: $13,850, if allowed.

## Itemized Deduction Indicators

Itemizing may help when the taxpayer has large qualified expenses, such as:

- charitable contributions
- uninsured medical and dental expenses
- mortgage interest and property taxes
- significant casualty or theft losses

## Bunching Strategy

When itemized deductions are close to the standard deduction, the taxpayer may bunch deductible payments into one year to exceed the standard deduction in that year.
