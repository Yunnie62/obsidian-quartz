# Corporate Capital Losses

## Core Rule

Corporate capital losses can offset only corporate capital gains. They cannot offset ordinary income.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：个人和公司的 capital loss 规则不同，公司 capital loss 不能直接抵 ordinary income。
