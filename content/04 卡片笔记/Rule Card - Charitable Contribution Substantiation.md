# Charitable Contribution Substantiation

## Core Rule

Noncash charitable contributions may require additional documentation.

## Key Requirements

- More than $500 of noncash property: file Form 8283.
- More than $5,000 for one item or group of similar items: generally obtain a written qualified appraisal.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：大额 noncash donation 不只是算 deduction，还要满足 substantiation。
