# Unrelated Business Income (UBI)

## Core Rule

UBI is income from a trade or business that is regularly carried on and not substantially related to the organization's exempt purpose. It may be taxable even though the organization is tax-exempt.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：判断 UBI 三步：trade/business、regularly carried on、not related to exempt purpose。
