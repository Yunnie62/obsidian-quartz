# S Corporation Termination

## Core Rule

S corporation status can terminate by voluntary revocation, failure to meet S corporation eligibility requirements, or excessive passive investment income when the corporation has accumulated C corporation E&P.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：excess passive investment income 可能影响 S status，尤其是有 C corp E&P 的 S corp。
