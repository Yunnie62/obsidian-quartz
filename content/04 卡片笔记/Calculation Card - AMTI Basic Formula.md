# AMTI Basic Formula

## Core Formula

Regular taxable income  
+ AMT adjustments  
+ AMT preference items  
= Alternative minimum taxable income (AMTI)

## Adjustments

Adjustments exist because AMT rules treat certain income or deduction items differently from regular tax rules. Some are timing differences, while others permanently disallow certain regular tax benefits for AMT purposes.

## Preference Items

Preference items increase AMTI. They are generally tax benefits allowed for regular tax but not allowed for AMT.

## Exam Approach

1. Start with regular taxable income.
2. Identify AMT adjustments.
3. Identify AMT preference items.
4. Add them to compute AMTI.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：AMTI 不是从 gross income 重新开始算，而是从 regular taxable income 开始调整。
