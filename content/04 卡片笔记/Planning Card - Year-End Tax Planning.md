# Year-End Tax Planning

## Core Idea

Year-end tax planning often combines timing, deductions, withholding, retirement or health contributions, and capital gain planning to reduce tax or avoid penalties.

## Common Planning Moves

- Defer income when future tax cost is lower in present value.
- Accelerate deductions when current-year tax savings are more valuable.
- Bunch itemized deductions to exceed the standard deduction.
- Contribute to tax-favored accounts when eligible.
- Review withholding and estimated payments before year end.
- Consider capital gain timing and holding period.

## Exam Approach

1. Compute the baseline tax result.
2. Apply the planning move.
3. Recompute taxable income, tax, and payments.
4. Check penalties or safe harbors.
5. Compare after-tax outcomes.
