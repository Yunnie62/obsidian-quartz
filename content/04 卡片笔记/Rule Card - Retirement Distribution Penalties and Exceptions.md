# Retirement Distribution Penalties and Exceptions

## Core Rule

Premature taxable retirement distributions may be subject to a 10% additional penalty tax before age 59 1/2, unless an exception applies.

## Common Exceptions

- Disability
- Death
- Certain medical expenses
- Qualified education expenses for IRAs
- First-time homebuyer IRA distribution, subject to a $10,000 limit
- Certain birth or adoption distributions
- Qualified domestic relations order for certain plans
