# Section 529 Qualified Tuition Programs

## Core Rule

Section 529 plans are tax-favored education funding arrangements. Distributions are tax-free to the extent used for qualified education expenses.

## Types

- Prepaid tuition plans
- Educational savings plans

## Taxable Portion

If distributions exceed qualified expenses, the earnings portion of the excess distribution may be taxable and may also be subject to an additional 10% penalty.
