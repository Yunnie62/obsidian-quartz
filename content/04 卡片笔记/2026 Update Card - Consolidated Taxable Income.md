# 2026 Update - Consolidated Taxable Income

## Calculation Steps

1. Calculate each member's stand-alone taxable income.
2. Remove intercompany transaction effects.
3. Remove items determined at the consolidated level.
4. Combine member taxable incomes.
5. Apply consolidated-level items.

## Consolidated-Level Items

- Capital gains and losses
- Section 1231 gains and losses
- NOL
- Charitable contribution deduction
- DRD

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：intercompany items are removed before consolidated-level items are applied.
