# Corporate NOL Utilization

## Core Rule

Corporate NOL usage depends on the year the NOL arose. Post-2017 NOL carryforwards are generally carried forward indefinitely, but beginning in 2021 they can offset only 80% of taxable income after pre-2018 NOL carryforwards.
