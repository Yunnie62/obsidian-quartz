# Substantial Presence Test

## Core Rule

The substantial presence test can cause a foreign individual to be treated as a U.S. resident for tax purposes based on days of presence in the United States.

## Exam Approach

1. Count current-year U.S. days.
2. Apply the weighted multi-year day formula when required.
3. Check exceptions or treaty relief.
