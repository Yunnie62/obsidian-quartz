# Boot and Liabilities in Corporate Formation

## Core Rule

Boot received in a Section 351 transaction can trigger recognized gain. Liabilities assumed by the corporation can also trigger gain when liabilities exceed the adjusted basis of transferred assets.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：recognized gain is limited, but liabilities in excess of basis can still create gain.
