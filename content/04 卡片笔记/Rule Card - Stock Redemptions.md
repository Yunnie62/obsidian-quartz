# Stock Redemptions

## Core Rule

When a corporation buys back shareholder stock, the redemption may be treated as a sale or exchange, or as a dividend to the extent of E&P.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：redemption 不一定是 sale treatment，要判断是否符合 sale/exchange 条件。
