# Foreign-Earned Income Exclusion

## Core Rule

A qualifying taxpayer may exclude a limited amount of foreign-earned income from U.S. gross income.

## Key Requirements

- The income must be foreign-earned income.
- The taxpayer must have a tax home in a foreign country.
- The taxpayer must satisfy either the bona fide residence test or the physical presence test.

## Physical Presence Test

The taxpayer must be physically present in one or more foreign countries for at least 330 full days during any 12-consecutive-month period.

## 2023 Amount

- Maximum exclusion: $120,000.

## Exam Approach

1. Confirm the income is earned income.
2. Confirm the income is earned while working outside the United States.
3. Test qualification.
4. Apply the exclusion limit.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：先判断 qualification，再用 exclusion amount。不要先套 $120,000。
