# The tax timing of equity compensation depends on the award type and key dates

## Core Rule

For equity compensation questions, do not calculate tax first. First identify the award type and the key dates: grant date, vesting date, exercise date, and sale date. Different awards, such as stock options, restricted stock, and SARs, trigger ordinary income, basis adjustments, and capital gain or loss at different points.

## Exam Approach

- Identify the award type.
- Determine the taxable event.
- Classify the income.
- Calculate basis and later gain or loss on sale.

## Chinese Note

- 易错提醒：不要看到 stock 就直接按 capital gain 处理，很多股权薪酬先产生 ordinary compensation income。
