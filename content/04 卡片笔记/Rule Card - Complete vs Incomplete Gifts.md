# Complete vs Incomplete Gifts

## Core Rule

Only complete gifts are subject to gift tax and eligible for the annual gift tax exclusion.

## Complete Gift

A gift is complete when the donor has given up sufficient control over the property.

## Incomplete Gift

A gift is incomplete if the donor retains meaningful power to revoke, alter, or control the transfer.
